# High school

Algebra 1 and 2, functions, polynomials, exponentials and logarithms, geometry proofs, trigonometry, vectors and matrices, sequences, series and counting. Grades 9 to 12.

Before this folder: Middle school.

The diagnostic quiz and the cheat sheet for this folder are written together with its lessons.
