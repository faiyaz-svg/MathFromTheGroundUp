# High school diagnostic

This quiz covers the 44 lessons of the high school folder: two questions on each of the 42 core lessons and one on each of the 2 optional lessons (conic sections, and the law of sines and cosines), 86 questions in all. Take it without notes and without a calculator, and write every answer down before you look at the answers. Most questions take a minute or two, so plan on four or five sittings rather than one.

Mark each answer right, wrong or guessed. A guess counts as wrong, even when it turns out right. Then do only the lessons behind the questions you marked wrong or guessed; the answer table names the lesson for each question. If you skip the two optional lessons, nothing later depends on them.

## Algebra foundations

1\. Let $A = \{x \in \mathbb{Z} : -2 \le x < 3\}$. List the elements of $A$. Then say which of these are true: $2 \in A$, $\{2\} \subseteq A$, $\{2\} \in A$.

2\. Which property of the real numbers justifies $(7 + 2) + x = 7 + (2 + x)$? Then explain why $0 \cdot 3 = 0 \cdot 5$ does not let you cancel the 0 to get $3 = 5$.

3\. Solve $\frac{2x - 1}{3} - \frac{x + 2}{4} = 1$.

4\. Solve $A = P(1 + rt)$ for $t$, and solve $a - bp = c + dp$ for $p$. State what must be nonzero.

5\. Solve $-3 \le 2x + 1 < 7$ and write the answer in interval notation.

6\. Solve $1 - 2x > 5$ or $3x \ge 3$ in interval notation. Then write "$x < 1$ and $x > 3$" as a set.

7\. Solve $\lvert 2x - 3 \rvert = 7$ and $\lvert x \rvert = -4$.

8\. Solve $\lvert x + 1 \rvert - 3 \ge 2$ in interval notation.

## Linear functions and systems

9\. Write the line through $(-2, 5)$ with slope 3 in point-slope form and in standard form $Ax + By = C$ with $A \ge 0$.

10\. Find the slope of $2x + 3y = 12$. Then write, in standard form, the line through $(1, 2)$ perpendicular to it.

11\. Solve the system $x + y + z = 6$, $2x - y + z = 3$ and $x + 2y - z = 2$.

12\. How many solutions does $x + 2y = 3$ have together with (a) $2x + 4y = 6$ and (b) $2x + 4y = 7$? Then find the equilibrium price $P$ and quantity $Q$ when $Q_d = 150 - 10P$ and $Q_s = 30 + 20P$.

13\. For $2x - y > 4$, is the boundary line solid or dashed, and does the shaded side contain the origin? Is $(3, 1)$ a solution?

14\. Maximize $P = 3x + 2y$ subject to $x + y \le 4$, $x \le 3$, $x \ge 0$ and $y \ge 0$.

## Functions

15\. Find the natural domain of $f(x) = \frac{\sqrt{x + 1}}{x - 3}$.

16\. Let $f(x) = x^2 + 1$ with domain and codomain $\mathbb{R}$. Find its range. Are $\frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 2}$ and $x + 2$ the same function?

17\. Find the average rate of change of $f(x) = x^2$ from $x = -1$ to $x = 3$, and from $x = -1$ to $x = 1$. What does the second answer not mean?

18\. Decide whether each function is even, odd or neither: $x^4 - 3x^2$, $x^3 + x$ and $x^2 + x$.

19\. Let $f(x) = x + 3$ for $x < 1$ and $f(x) = 2x$ for $x \ge 1$. Find $f(-2)$, $f(1)$ and $f(3)$, and solve $f(x) = 4$.

20\. Work out $\lfloor -3.7 \rfloor$, $\lceil -3.7 \rceil$ and $\lceil 5 \rceil$. How many pages are needed for 47 items at 10 per page, written with a ceiling?

21\. Describe how $g(x) = 3f(2x - 6) + 1$ is obtained from the graph of $f$. Where does the point $(4, 5)$ on $f$ go?

22\. Does $y = f(x + 2)$ shift the graph of $f$ left or right? Write the equation for $y = \sqrt{x}$ reflected in the $y$-axis and then shifted right 2.

23\. Let $f(x) = 2x + 1$ and $g(x) = x^2$. Find $f(g(3))$, $g(f(3))$ and a formula for $(g \circ f)(x)$.

24\. Find the inverse of $f(x) = 3x - 5$. Does $f(x) = x^2$ have an inverse function on all of $\mathbb{R}$? If not, on which domain does the inverse $\sqrt{x}$ work?

## Radicals and polynomials

## Exponentials and logarithms

49\. A population of 500 doubles every 3 years. How large is it after 12 years? What multiplier describes a 15% fall each year?

50\. An exponential function $f(x) = a \cdot b^x$ passes through $(1, 6)$ and $(3, 24)$. Find $a$ and $b$.

51\. Work out $\log_2 \frac{1}{8}$, $\log_4 32$ and $\log_7 1$.

52\. Find the domain of $f(x) = \log_3(x - 2)$. How many bits are needed to give 1000 distinct items different IDs?

54\. Given $\log_{10} 2 \approx 0.301$, find $\log_{10} 8$ and $\log_{10} 5$. Is $\log_{10}(2 + 3)$ equal to $\log_{10} 2 + \log_{10} 3$?

56\. Solve $\log_2 x + \log_2(x - 2) = 3$. Then use the rule of 72 to estimate the years for money to double at 6% a year.

## Geometry and proof

57\. Write the converse and the contrapositive of "If a whole number is divisible by 4, then it is even". Which of the three statements are true?

58\. Two angles form a linear pair and measure $(3x + 10)^\circ$ and $(5x - 6)^\circ$. Find $x$ and the two angles.

59\. Two triangles have two pairs of equal sides and one pair of equal angles, and the angle is not between the two sides. Does that prove congruence? When may you use CPCTC?

60\. In $\triangle ABC$ with $AB = AC$, the angle at $B$ is $(3x + 5)^\circ$ and the angle at $C$ is $(5x - 15)^\circ$. Find $x$ and $\angle A$.

61\. A 1.5 m stick casts a 2 m shadow at the same time as a tree casts a 14 m shadow. Name the similarity test and find the height of the tree.

62\. In $\triangle ABC$, the point $D$ is on $AB$ and $E$ is on $AC$ with $DE \parallel BC$. If $AD = 4$, $DB = 6$, $AE = 6$ and $BC = 15$, find $EC$ and $DE$.

63\. Find the distance, the midpoint and the slope for the points $(-3, 2)$ and $(5, -4)$.

64\. Write $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 4y - 12 = 0$ in the form $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$. Then find the tangent line at the point $(6, 2)$ on the circle.

65\. (Optional lesson.) For the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$, find $a$, $b$, $c$ and the foci.

## Trigonometry

66\. In a right triangle with legs 5 and 12, find $\sin\theta$, $\cos\theta$ and $\tan\theta$ for the angle $\theta$ opposite the side of length 5.

67\. From 50 m away from a tower, the angle of elevation to its top is $45^\circ$ and your eye is 1.5 m above the ground. How tall is the tower?

68\. Convert $150^\circ$ to radians and $\frac{7\pi}{4}$ to degrees. Find the arc length for radius 6 and angle $\frac{5\pi}{6}$.

69\. Find $\sin\frac{5\pi}{6}$ and $\tan\frac{7\pi}{4}$. If $\sin\theta = \frac{3}{5}$ and $\theta$ is in quadrant II, find $\cos\theta$.

70\. For $y = 2\sin(3x - \pi) + 1$, find the amplitude, period, phase shift, midline, maximum and minimum.

71\. A tide has a high of 5 m and a low of 1 m, with a period of 12 hours and a high tide at $t = 0$. Write a cosine model for the height $y$ at time $t$.

72\. Use a sum formula to find the exact value of $\sin 75^\circ$. Then test the claim $\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta$ at $\theta = 30^\circ$.

73\. Solve $2\sin x\cos x = \sin x$ on $[0, 2\pi)$. Then find $\sin^{-1}\left(\sin\frac{5\pi}{6}\right)$.

74\. (Optional lesson.) A triangle has sides $a = 7$ and $b = 8$ with the angle $C = 60^\circ$ between them. Find the side $c$ and the area.

## Vectors and matrices

75\. For $\mathbf{u} = (3, -1)$ and $\mathbf{v} = (-2, 5)$, find $\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}$ and $3\mathbf{u} - \mathbf{v}$. Then find the unit vector in the direction of $(3, 4)$.

76\. Find $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{w}$ for $\mathbf{u} = (1, 2)$ and $\mathbf{w} = (4, -2)$ and say what it shows. Then find $\lVert (3, 0) + (0, 4) \rVert$.

77\. With $A$ having rows $(2, 1)$ and $(-1, 3)$ and $B$ having rows $(1, 0)$ and $(4, -2)$, find $AB$ and $BA$.

78\. The matrix $A$ has rows $(3, 1)$ and $(5, 2)$. Find $\det A$ and $A^{-1}$, then solve $3x + y = 5$, $5x + 2y = 9$.

## Sequences, series and counting

79\. An arithmetic sequence (indexed from 0) has $a_3 = 11$ and $a_7 = 23$. Find the common difference and a formula for $a_n$.

80\. Let $h_0 = 0$ and $h_{n+1} = 2h_n + 1$. Find $h_1$ to $h_5$, guess a formula, and say what is needed to be sure it is right.

81\. Work out $\sum_{k=1}^{100} k$ and $\sum_{k=3}^{10} (2k + 1)$.

82\. Work out $\sum_{k=0}^{9} 3 \cdot 2^k$, and write $0.\overline{36}$ as a fraction in lowest terms.

83\. How many 4-digit PINs use no digit twice? How many committees of 3 can be chosen from 8 people?

84\. How many distinct arrangements of the letters of BANANA are there? How many bit strings of length 6 contain at least one 1?

85\. Expand $(2x - 1)^4$.

86\. Find the coefficient of $x^3$ in $(x + 2)^6$, and the sum of all entries in row 10 of Pascal's triangle.
