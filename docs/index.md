# Math From the Ground Up

A self-study math textbook that starts at grade 1 arithmetic and ends at university math, with side tracks for proofs, computer science math, economics and Canadian personal finance. Each lesson explains one idea in plain language, works through examples step by step, and ends with practice problems that have full solutions.

If you are new here, read [How to use this book](00-start-here/index.md) first. It explains the quick checks, the diagnostic quizzes and how to skip what you already know.

The companion book [CS From the Ground Up](https://faiyaz-svg.github.io/CSFromTheGroundUp/) covers computers, programming, data, the cloud and AI-assisted development in the same style.

## Where to begin

Short on time? Take the diagnostic quiz at the start of each folder and do only the lessons behind the questions you miss. Work through the proofs and logic folder in full, since university courses lean on it the most.
