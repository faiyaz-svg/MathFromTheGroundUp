# Middle school

Negative numbers, ratios and percents, equations and inequalities, exponents and roots, linear relationships, geometry, and first statistics. Grades 6 to 8.

Before this folder: Elementary.

## How to use this folder

Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md) first, without notes or a calculator. It asks two questions on each of the 29 lessons, and its answer table names the lesson to study for each one you miss, so you can do only those lessons and skip the rest. Each chapter has its own cheat sheet (the links are below), and each lesson starts with a quick check, so you can test yourself before reading it.

The [Anki deck for this folder](../downloads/anki/02-middle-school.apkg) holds the facts and rules that are easy to mix up, such as the sign rules, the order of operations, the exponent rules, the percent change multipliers, the slope formula and the area and volume formulas. In Anki desktop choose File, then Import, and [Anki decks](../00-start-here/index.md#anki-decks) covers phones.

## Fast path for a CS student

If you are short on time, these lessons carry the most weight in the later proofs, CS math and economics folders. They are listed in reading order, so each one only needs the ones above it.

| Lesson | Where it comes back |
|---|---|
| [Integer arithmetic and sign rules](01-integers-and-rational-numbers/02-integer-arithmetic-and-sign-rules.md) | every calculation with signed values, and the remainder of a negative number in code |
| [Exponents and order of operations](01-integers-and-rational-numbers/04-exponents-and-order-of-operations.md) | reading and writing formulas, and what `-3**2` means in each tool |
| [Percent change and applications](02-ratios-rates-percents/04-percent-change-and-applications.md) | growth, inflation, returns and the multiplier method used in finance |
| [Equivalent expressions](03-expressions-equations-inequalities/02-equivalent-expressions.md) | every algebra step in a proof, and checking that a rewrite is legal |
| [Multi-step equations](03-expressions-equations-inequalities/04-multi-step-equations.md) | solving for a variable in a formula, from break-even points to interest rates |
| [Inequalities](03-expressions-equations-inequalities/05-inequalities.md) | loop bounds, constraints and the limit definitions in calculus |
| [Exponent rules](04-powers-roots-and-real-numbers/01-exponent-rules.md) | powers of 2, logarithms, running times and compound growth |
| [Scientific notation](04-powers-roots-and-real-numbers/02-scientific-notation.md) | orders of magnitude, SI prefixes and binary storage units |
| [Roots, irrational numbers and types of numbers](04-powers-roots-and-real-numbers/03-roots-irrationals-and-types-of-numbers.md) | a worked proof by contradiction, and the sets $\mathbb{N}$, $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$ |
| [Slope and linear equations](05-linear-relationships-and-functions/02-slope-and-linear-equations.md) | rates of change, marginal cost and the derivative |
| [Introduction to functions](05-linear-relationships-and-functions/03-intro-to-functions.md) | $f(x)$ notation, domain and range, and pure functions in programs |
| [Systems of linear equations](05-linear-relationships-and-functions/04-systems-of-linear-equations.md) | supply and demand, break-even and linear algebra |
| [Basic probability](07-statistics-and-probability/02-basic-probability.md) | counting outcomes, independence and the start of statistics |

The geometry chapter matters less for the CS and economics work, apart from the [Pythagorean theorem](06-geometry/04-pythagorean-theorem.md), which gives distance in the plane.

## Cheat sheet

Each chapter has a cheat sheet with the rules, tables and common mistakes of its lessons. This is the map.

- [Integers and rational numbers](01-integers-and-rational-numbers/index.md#cheat-sheet) covers absolute value, the sign rules, fractions and repeating decimals, and the order of operations.
- [Ratios, rates and percents](02-ratios-rates-percents/index.md#cheat-sheet) covers unit rates, proportions, percents of a whole, and percent change with multipliers.
- [Expressions, equations and inequalities](03-expressions-equations-inequalities/index.md#cheat-sheet) covers terms and like terms, expanding and factoring, one-step to multi-step equations, and inequalities.
- [Powers, roots and real numbers](04-powers-roots-and-real-numbers/index.md#cheat-sheet) covers the exponent rules, scientific notation, roots and irrational numbers, and estimation.
- [Linear relationships and functions](05-linear-relationships-and-functions/index.md#cheat-sheet) covers the coordinate plane, slope and intercepts, function notation, and systems of equations.
- [Geometry](06-geometry/index.md#cheat-sheet) covers angle facts, area, volume, the Pythagorean theorem, and transformations, congruence and similarity.
- [Statistics and probability](07-statistics-and-probability/index.md#cheat-sheet) covers mean, median and spread, probability rules, scatter plots and two-way tables.
