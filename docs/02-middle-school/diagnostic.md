# Middle school diagnostic

This quiz covers the 29 lessons of the middle school folder, with two questions on each. Take it without notes and without a calculator, and write every answer down before you look at the answers. Most questions take a minute or two, and the whole quiz is best done in two or three sittings.

Mark each answer right, wrong or guessed. A guess counts as wrong, even when it turns out right. Then do only the lessons behind the questions you marked wrong or guessed; the answer table names the lesson for each question.

## Integers and rational numbers

1\. Put these in order from smallest to largest: $-27$, $-4$, $0$, $-3.5$, $2$.

2\. Work out $\lvert 3 - 8 \rvert$ and $\lvert 3 \rvert - \lvert 8 \rvert$. Then solve $\lvert x \rvert = 7$.

3\. Work out $-8 + (-5)$ and $6 - (-9)$.

4\. Work out $(-2)(-3)(-5)$. Then find the change in temperature from $-14^\circ\text{C}$ to $-2^\circ\text{C}$.

5\. Work out $-\frac{5}{6} + \frac{3}{4}$.

6\. Write $0.\overline{27}$ as a fraction in simplest form, and decide whether $\frac{21}{56}$ has a terminating decimal.

7\. Work out $-3^2$, $(-3)^2$ and $8 \div 2 \times 4$.

8\. Work out $2 + 3 \times (10 - 4)^2 \div 6$ and $-7^0$.

## Ratios, rates and percents

9\. Ana and Ben share \$420 in the ratio $3:5$. How much does each get?

10\. A 250 g box of cereal costs \$3.50 and a 400 g box costs \$5.20. Which is cheaper per 100 g?

11\. A recipe uses 3 cups of flour for 20 cookies. How many cups are needed for 50 cookies?

12\. A taxi charges \$4.50 plus \$2 per km. Find the fares for 5 km and for 10 km, and say whether the fare is proportional to the distance.

13\. A seminar has 12 women and 18 men. What percent of the seminar are women?

14\. A bill with 13% HST included comes to \$113. What was the price before tax?

15\. A price rises from \$40 to \$50. Find the percent change. Then find the percent change when it falls from \$50 back to \$40.

16\. A \$200 coat is 40% off, and then 25% off the sale price. Find the final price.

## Expressions, equations and inequalities

17\. Evaluate $x^2 - 3x + 1$ and $-x^2$ when $x = -3$.

18\. Write expressions for "5 less than twice $n$" and "twice the sum of $n$ and 5".

19\. Simplify $4(2x - 3) - (x - 5)$.

20\. Factor $18x^2 - 12x + 6$. Then decide whether $x^2 + 2$ and $3x$ are equivalent expressions.

21\. Solve $3x + 11 = 2$ and $\frac{2}{3}x = 10$.

22\. A gym charges a \$25 joining fee plus \$18 per month. Kamal has paid \$187 in total. Write an equation and find the number of months.

23\. Solve $2(x - 3) + 4 = 5x - 14$.

24\. Say how many solutions $3(x + 2) = 3x + 6$ has, and how many $2x + 1 = 2x + 5$ has.

25\. Solve $-2x + 5 \ge 13$.

26\. A phone plan costs \$20 plus \$3 per GB, and you can spend at most \$45. Write an inequality and find the most whole GB you can use.

## Powers, roots and real numbers

27\. Simplify $(2x^3)^4 \cdot x^{-5}$.

28\. Work out $\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^{-2}$ and $2^{-1} + 3^{-1}$.

29\. Write 0.00052 and 93,000,000 in scientific notation.

30\. Work out $(6 \times 10^4)(5 \times 10^{-7})$ and $4.5 \times 10^6 + 3 \times 10^4$, each in scientific notation.

31\. Simplify $\sqrt{72}$, and work out $\sqrt{9 + 16}$.

32\. Which of $\sqrt{49}$, $0.\overline{3}$, $\sqrt{8}$, $\frac{22}{7}$ and $\pi$ are rational numbers?

33\. Estimate $\frac{387 \times 5.2}{0.49}$ by rounding each number to one significant figure.

34\. A calculator shows $48 \times 53 = 25{,}440$. Without multiplying exactly, show that this is wrong, and say about how big the product should be.

## Linear relationships and functions

35\. Name the quadrant or axis that holds each point: $(-4, -1)$, $(0, 5)$ and $(2, -3)$.

36\. Reflect $(3, -2)$ in the $y$-axis, and find the distance from $(-3, 2)$ to $(4, 2)$.

37\. Find the slope of the line through $(-1, 7)$ and $(3, -1)$, and write its equation in the form $y = mx + b$.

38\. For the line $2x + 3y = 12$, find the $x$-intercept, the $y$-intercept and the slope.

39\. If $g(x) = 2x^2 - 3x + 1$, find $g(-2)$.

40\. Decide whether each is a function with input $x$ and output $y$: (a) the pairs $(1, 5)$, $(2, 6)$, $(2, 7)$ and $(3, 8)$ written as $(x, y)$; (b) $y = x^2$; (c) $x = y^2$.

41\. Solve the system $2x + 3y = 12$ and $3x - 2y = 5$.

42\. How many solutions does each system have? (a) $2x + 4y = 8$ and $x + 2y = 4$; (b) $y = 3x + 1$ and $y = 3x - 2$.

## Geometry

43\. Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, and one of a pair of co-interior angles is $65^\circ$. Find the other angle. Then find the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon.

44\. Can sides of length 2, 3 and 10 form a triangle? If two sides of a triangle are 5 and 8, between which two numbers must the third side lie?

45\. A circle has a diameter of 10 cm. Find its area and its circumference, leaving $\pi$ in the answers.

46\. Find the area of a trapezoid with parallel sides of 6 cm and 10 cm and a height of 4 cm, and the area of a $90^\circ$ sector of a circle with radius 6 cm.

47\. A cylinder has a radius of 3 cm and a height of 10 cm. Find its volume and its total surface area, leaving $\pi$ in the answers.

48\. Doubling every length of a solid multiplies its surface area by what, and its volume by what? Then find the volume of a cone with radius 3 cm and height 4 cm.

49\. A 13 m ladder leans on a wall with its foot 5 m from the wall. How high up the wall does it reach?

50\. Find the distance from $(-3, 2)$ to $(5, -4)$, and decide whether a triangle with sides 7, 24 and 25 has a right angle.

51\. The point $(2, 3)$ is rotated $90^\circ$ counterclockwise about the origin. Where does it land? Where does it land when reflected in the line $y = x$ instead?

52\. In $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$, the sides are $AB = 6$, $DE = 15$ and $BC = 8$. Find $EF$ and the ratio of the area of $\triangle DEF$ to the area of $\triangle ABC$.

## Statistics and probability

53\. Find the mean, the median and the mode of 4, 6, 6, 8, 10, 20.

54\. Find $Q_1$, $Q_3$ and the IQR of 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 11, 12, 30, using the book's method, and decide whether 30 is an outlier.

55\. Two fair dice are rolled. Find the probability that the sum is 7. Then find the probability of at least one six in 3 rolls of one die.

56\. Two marbles are drawn without replacement from a bag with 2 red and 3 blue marbles. Find the probability that both are red.

57\. The points $(2, 55)$ and $(6, 75)$ lie on a line of best fit for test score $y$ (out of 100) against study hours $x$. Find the equation of the line, interpret its slope, and explain why it should not be used at $x = 20$.

58\. In a group of 200 students, the counts are as in the table. What percent of the CS students use Linux, and what percent of the Linux users are CS students?

| | Uses Linux | Does not use Linux |
|---|---|---|
| CS student | 30 | 20 |
| Not CS | 10 | 140 |
