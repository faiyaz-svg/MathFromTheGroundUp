# Elementary diagnostic

This quiz covers the 16 lessons of the elementary folder, with two questions on each. Take it without notes and without a calculator, and write every answer down before you look at the answers. Most questions take under a minute.

Mark each answer right, wrong or guessed. A guess counts as wrong, even when it turns out right. Then do only the lessons behind the questions you marked wrong or guessed; the answer table names the lesson for each question.

## Place value and rounding

1\. Write "five million sixty thousand eight" in digits.

2\. Round 39,961 to the nearest hundred, and round 2,449 to the nearest thousand.

## Addition and subtraction

3\. Work out $7{,}005 - 2{,}368$.

4\. Work out $72 - 39$ in your head.

## Multiplication and times tables

5\. Answer from memory: $7 \times 8$, $6 \times 7$, $9 \times 6$, $8 \times 8$ and $12 \times 12$. If you had to rebuild a fact from a rule, mark it guessed.

6\. Work out $40 \times 50$ and $305 \times 100$.

## Properties of operations

7\. Work out $25 \times 17 \times 4$ in your head, and name the properties that let you do it the easy way.

8\. Work out $6 \times 98$ with the distributive property. Then decide whether $(48 \div 4) \div 2$ equals $48 \div (4 \div 2)$.

## Multi-digit multiplication

9\. Work out $307 \times 46$.

10\. A classmate works out $52 \times 46$ and gets 520. Find the mistake and the right answer.

## Division facts and remainders

11\. Fifty people ride in vans that seat 8. How many vans are needed? If instead \$50 is split equally among 8 people, how much does each get?

12\. Use the divisibility rules to decide which of 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 10 divide 5,124.

## Long division

13\. Work out $4{,}242 \div 6$.

14\. Find the quotient and remainder when 2,589 is divided by 21.

## Factors, multiples and primes

15\. Write 252 as a product of primes, and decide whether 91 is prime.

16\. Find the greatest common factor and the least common multiple of 20 and 48.

## Fractions and equivalent fractions

17\. Write $\frac{72}{96}$ in simplest form.

18\. Which is bigger, $\frac{4}{7}$ or $\frac{5}{9}$? Write $4\frac{2}{3}$ as an improper fraction.

## Adding and subtracting fractions

19\. Work out $\frac{5}{6} + \frac{3}{10}$.

20\. Work out $7\frac{2}{5} - 3\frac{3}{4}$.

## Multiplying and dividing fractions

21\. Work out $\frac{9}{14} \times \frac{7}{12}$ in simplest form.

22\. Work out $3\frac{1}{3} \div \frac{5}{6}$.

## Decimal place value, adding and subtracting

23\. Write 0.35 as a fraction in simplest form. Then put 0.3, 0.29, 0.305 and 0.03 in order from smallest to largest.

24\. Work out $15 - 6.38$ and $4.7 + 0.85$.

## Multiplying and dividing decimals

25\. Work out $0.3 \times 0.4$ and $2.4 \times 0.15$.

26\. Work out $9.36 \div 0.12$, and write $\frac{4}{11}$ as a decimal.

## Powers of ten

27\. Work out $0.068 \times 1000$ and $52 \div 1000$.

28\. Write $10^4$ and $10^{-3}$ as ordinary numbers, and work out $7.5 \div 0.01$.

## Measurement and unit conversion

29\. Convert 3,250 g to kilograms, and $2.5\ \text{m}^2$ to square centimetres.

30\. Convert 54 km/h to metres per second, and write 2.3 h in hours and minutes.

## Geometry basics

31\. A parallelogram has a base of 10 cm, slanted sides of 6 cm and a height of 5 cm. Find its perimeter and its area.

32\. Find the area of a triangle with a base of 9 cm and a height of 4 cm, and the volume, in litres, of a box 50 cm long, 20 cm wide and 30 cm tall.

## What to do next

List the lessons behind every question you marked wrong or guessed, and do them in order, since later lessons build on earlier ones. Start each lesson with its quick check: if you get all three right, go straight to its practice problems. If you missed nothing, go on to [02-middle-school](../02-middle-school/index.md). Use the [cheat sheet](index.md#cheat-sheet) for review, and take this diagnostic again a few weeks after you finish the lessons, as [how to study](../00-start-here/how-to-study.md#use-the-diagnostics-well) recommends.
